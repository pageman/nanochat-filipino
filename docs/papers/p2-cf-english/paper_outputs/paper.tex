\begin{abstract}
Pajo (2026) trained nanochat from scratch on WikiText-TL-39 and measured held-out Tagalog bits-per-byte (BPB) under equal exposure (AsPredicted \#306780). That study is complete and is not amended here. Those Tagalog-only checkpoints never saw English, so they cannot answer whether a model ``forgot English because it learned Tagalog.'' This Stage~1 manuscript locks the complementary experiment, still entirely inside Karpathy nanochat: \texttt{tok\_train} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{base\_train} $\rightarrow$ official \texttt{evaluate\_bpb}, commit \texttt{92d63d4}, depth dial only. Pretrain on WikiText-103 raw (English-train 32{,}768 BPE, $T=2048$), prove provenance, then continue on the frozen P1.1 WikiText-TL-39 train split. Dual confirmatory benchmarks: English \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} (retention / forgetting) and Tagalog \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} on \texttt{reconstructed\_article\_70\_15\_15} (Filipino acquisition). The forgetting contrast is extra-English continuation versus Tagalog continuation at the same token budget. P1.1 Table~2 is the Tagalog from-scratch ceiling and the English negative control. New GitHub and Hub repositories; P1.1 weights stay read-only. No phase-2 numbers are invented. No Hugging Face Trainer, CORE, chat, or SFT in the primary table.
\end{abstract}

\section{What changed, and why P1.1 cannot be the CF parent}
\label{sec:flip}

The question is: after English pretraining, how much English is lost when the same decoder is continued on Filipino Wikipedia text, and did it actually learn Filipino?

WikiText-TL-39 is a corpus, not a model that was ``pretrained in English.'' English pretraining is a separate nanochat run on WikiText-103 raw~\cite{merity2017pointer}. Filipino tuning uses the frozen P1.1 train split of WikiText-TL-39~\cite{cruz2019eval,pajo2026p11}.

P1.1 Hub checkpoints are a pure Tagalog speedrun~\cite{karpathy2026nanochat,aspredicted306780}. Using them as the CF parent would measure Tagalog retention under English data --- the opposite causal arrow. That draft is retired.

P1.1 remains the measurement instrument for Filipino and the negative control for English (a model that was \emph{not} English-pretrained).

\section{Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=1.25em]
\item[P1.1] Frozen Tagalog-from-scratch study. Not the English parent.
\item[EN0] English-from-scratch nanochat on WikiText-103 raw. This \emph{is} the CF parent.
\item[P0] Provenance battery, scored before any Tagalog training token.
\item[A0] Frozen EN0, dual eval (English before, Tagalog before).
\item[A1] Extra English continuation, $D_{\mathrm{phase2}}$ (drift).
\item[A2] Tagalog continuation on P1.1 train, $D_{\mathrm{phase2}}$ (CF intervention).
\item[A3] 50/50 English/Tagalog documents, $D_{\mathrm{phase2}}$.
\item[$C_{\mathrm{en}}$] English \texttt{val\_bpb\_full}(A2)$-${}\texttt{val\_bpb\_full}(A1). Positive: Tagalog data hurt English more than more English did.
\item[Acquisition] Tagalog \texttt{val\_bpb\_full}(A2) versus P1.1 Table~2 (from-scratch ceiling) and versus A0 (English parent before Filipino).
\item[Collapse (English)] After A2, English val BPB $\ge$ EN0 untrained, or $\Delta_{\mathrm{en}}\ge 1.0$ versus A0, or English val BPB no longer beats the English byte unigram. Not a $0.02$ bump.
\item[BPB] Bits per UTF-8 byte. Fair across the English BPE and the P1.1 Tagalog BPE. Do not mix tokenizers in one training run.
\end{description}

\section{P1.1 facts used as references, not as parents}
\label{sec:p11}

Tagalog \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} seed 0: d8 $1.179135$, d12 $1.180824$, d16 $1.195546$, d20 $1.172248$. Untrained $\approx 3.289$; unigram $4.453225$. Split \texttt{reconstructed\_article\_70\_15\_15}. nanochat \texttt{92d63d4}. $T=2048$. One Tagalog test BPB $1.164768$ on d20; this study does not reuse it as a post-continuation number.

\section{Confirmatory question}
\label{sec:question}

File a new AsPredicted before EN0 confirmatory BPB (pilots allowed). Then:

\begin{quote}
When an English-pretrained nanochat (EN0), whose English provenance has passed P0, is continued for $D_{\mathrm{phase2}}$ tokens on WikiText-TL-39 train, does English held-out BPB rise more than under matched extra-English continuation, and does Tagalog held-out BPB fall toward the P1.1 from-scratch ceiling?
\end{quote}

Prediction: $C_{\mathrm{en}}>0$ by at least $0.01$ BPB at the registered depths. Tagalog A2 BPB will be strictly below A0 Tagalog BPB (learning Filipino) but need not match P1.1 d20 (different tokenizer, different init). Depth rankings of $C_{\mathrm{en}}$ require the $0.01$ rule and more than one seed. P0 failure aborts A2.

\section{Provenance battery P0}
\label{sec:p0}

``Was it really pretrained in English?'' is an empirical gate, not a README claim.

On a frozen English validation split, before any Tagalog train token:

\begin{enumerate}
\item EN0 English \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} $<$ same-depth untrained.
\item EN0 English \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} $<$ P1.1 d20 English \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} on the same UTF-8 (P1.1 Tagalog BPE), by $\ge 0.01$ BPB.
\item EN0 Tagalog \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} $>$ P1.1 d20 Tagalog \texttt{val\_bpb\_full} (has not learned Filipino yet), by $\ge 0.01$ BPB.
\item English-train BPE hashes recorded; train log names WikiText-103 only; no ClimbMix.
\end{enumerate}

If any check fails, EN0 is not an English parent.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Catastrophic interference is sequential overwriting of old knowledge~\cite{mccloskey1989catastrophic,french1999cf,goodfellow2013cf}. Typical LLM CF starts from an English (or multilingual) base and adds a new task~\cite{luo2023forgetting,ramasesh2021forgetscale}. This paper stays in that causal direction, but keeps P1.1's purity: WikiText-family corpora, nanochat depth dial, BPB rather than CORE~\cite{merity2017pointer,cruz2019eval,karpathy2026nanochat,pajo2026p11}. EWC and replay are later~\cite{kirkpatrick2017ewc,lopezpaz2017gem}. Equal-exposure arithmetic is copied from P1.1 so English pretraining is not a silent compute confound~\cite{kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022chinchilla,muennighoff2023dataconstrained}.

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Lineage}
New AsPredicted. New GitHub/Hub \texttt{pageman/nanochat-filipino-p2-en-then-tl}. P1.1 repos read-only. Authority: new PDF $>$ \texttt{DIRECTION-RECALIBRATION.md} $>$ this manuscript $>$ ledger $>$ deviation.

\subsection{nanochat-only contract}
Every trained weight in this study is a nanochat decoder from commit \texttt{92d63d4e8bb4df75c3b71618f31ddde2378b2bcd}, plus the documented \texttt{NANOCHAT\_DATA\_DIR} hook and no other model-file edits. English BPE: \texttt{python -m scripts.tok\_train} on WikiText-103 raw train. English pretrain, extra-English, Tagalog continuation, and joint mix: \texttt{python -m scripts.base\_train} with \texttt{--depth}, \texttt{--max-seq-len=2048}, \texttt{--core-metric-every=-1}. Both language scores: official \texttt{evaluate\_bpb} through a copy of \texttt{scripts/p1/gate\_j\_full\_bpb.py}. P1.1 d20 used as a negative control is the already-published nanochat checkpoint, not a retrained Hugging Face model. Forbidden: Hugging Face \texttt{Trainer} or \texttt{from\_pretrained} as the language model, llama.cpp, \texttt{python -m nanochat.dataset}, CORE, SFT, a second architecture, or loading P1.1 \texttt{model\_000294.pt} as the English parent.

\subsection{English phase (EN0)}
Corpus: WikiText-103 \emph{raw}. LF-only. Article-hash 70/15/15. Tokenizer: 32{,}768 BPE on English \emph{train} only. Trainer: nanochat \texttt{92d63d4}, $T=2048$, $B=65{,}536$, $N$ from English $D_{3x}=3\times T_{\mathrm{en,train}}$ with explicit \texttt{--num-iterations}, ratio never $-1$, CORE off. Depths: 8 and 20 required; 12 and 16 if budget. Seed 0 confirmatory for EN0; extra seeds later.

\subsection{Tagalog phase (A2)}
Data: frozen P1.1 train JSONL only (hash \texttt{2b0474c5\ldots}). Encode with the \emph{English} BPE. $D_{\mathrm{phase2}}=19{,}267{,}584$ English-BPE tokens unless the PDF names English $D_{3x}$. Fresh optimizer, peak LR $=0.3\times$ EN0 peak, warmup 14. Do not load P1.1 \texttt{model\_000294.pt} as the start weight.

\subsection{Dual evaluators}
Same \texttt{evaluate\_bpb} wrapper as P1.1, two data roots. English val never in Tagalog paths. Seal English $C_{\mathrm{en}}$ and Tagalog acquisition on validation with both tests unread. Then at most one English test and at most one Tagalog test, predeclared. Loop \texttt{eval-tokens=262144} is diagnostic.

\subsection{What is not done}
No ClimbMix. No \texttt{python -m nanochat.dataset}. No P1.1 weight as English parent. No overwrite of \texttt{p1-fixed-d20-3x}. No Hugging Face \texttt{Trainer}. No confirmatory SFT/CORE/EWC. No invented rater scores. No mixing tokenizers in one run.

\section{Analysis plan}
\label{sec:analysis}

Primary: $\bar C_{\mathrm{en}}$ at d8 and d20, seeds as registered, threshold $0.01$. Secondary: Tagalog A2 versus P1.1 Table~2 and versus A0; A3; collapse binary on English. Falsification of forgetting: $C_{\mathrm{en}}$ not material. Falsification of acquisition: Tagalog A2 not better than A0 by $0.01$. Neither revives a P1.1 depth ranking.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

P1.1 Tagalog table is cited, not rerun. EN0, P0, and A0--A3 cells are empty until the new PDF exists and the runs complete~\cite{simmons2011falsepositive,wagenmakers2012confirmatory,nosek2018prereg}.

\section{Discussion rules}
\label{sec:discussion}

If $C_{\mathrm{en}}$ is material and Tagalog A2 improved versus A0, the model learned Filipino and paid an English cost beyond extra English training. If Tagalog did not improve, it did not learn Filipino; do not call that English CF due to Filipino competence. If $C_{\mathrm{en}}\approx 0$ and Tagalog improved, sequential Tagalog at this budget did not overwrite English on BPB. P0 is what licenses the sentence ``pretrained in English.''

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

No EN0 numbers yet. English BPE on Tagalog text shreds Tagalog; BPB still compares bytes. WikiText-103 $\neq$ web English. P1.1 reconstructed split. One parent seed unless extra seeds run. Shared named entities across Wikipedias. No chat.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Paper 1 stays a pure Tagalog base. Paper 2 trains an English nanochat, proves provenance, continues on WikiText-TL-39, and reports English forgetting and Filipino acquisition on the same BPB instrument. That is the pipeline. It is not a rewrite of AsPredicted \#306780.
