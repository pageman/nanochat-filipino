# Audit of the Proposed P5 Multi-Seed Panel Registration

## Overall assessment

The proposed P5 registration is a **strong and scientifically coherent next study**. It does not merely repeat P4: it converts P4’s one-seed token-share result into a predeclared three-new-seed panel. Its design maintains the key P4 treatment definition—C3 as a fixed \(q_{\mathrm{TL}}=0.50\) **source-content-token-share** mixture—and adds the next construct the program needs: a controlled descriptive assessment of whether P4’s two co-primary patterns recur across newly initialized Tagalog parents.

The central framing is correct: P5 is **post-P4**, **outcome-informed**, and **prospective only for its unobserved P5 outcomes**. It must not be described as outcome-independent, an amendment, a rescue, or a population-effect study. It is scientifically stronger than a one-new-seed P5 because its predeclared panel has no early stopping, no replacement seed, and no per-seed unblinding. The correct high-level claim after execution will be a transparent three-seed panel description—not a confidence interval, statistical significance test, or universal mixture law.

> **Bottom-line verdict:** Approve in principle, but make the pre-filing precision edits listed below. The highest-priority corrections concern the implementation of the new initialization seeds, the P0-T untrained-floor policy, panel-wide lockbox mechanics, and the formal status of technical failures. None requires changing the scientific question or adding a new arm.

## What the proposed P5 is

P5 is not the single-new-seed replication previously recommended as the narrowest possible successor to P4. It deliberately takes the next step further: a **predeclared three-seed panel** with parent-initialization seeds \(1,2,3\). That is a defensible choice because P4’s own limitations reserve multi-seed work for a separate P5 filing. The panel remains bounded because it locks the number of new seeds before any P5 parent, child, validation, or test score exists.

The proposed study contains four important commitments:

| Element | Proposed P5 commitment | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific treatment | P4 C1/C2/C3 branch geometry, including C3’s source-content token share \(q_{\mathrm{TL}}=0.50\) and the fixed P4 packed stream. | Correct replication target. |
| Fresh factor | New Tagalog parent trajectories at pre-named seeds 1, 2, and 3; no earlier-study weights as parents. | Correct new experimental factor, subject to a seed-implementation clarification. |
| Primary evidence | Six full-validation BPB cells per eligible seed; \(R_{\mathrm{TL}}(s)\) and \(A_{\mathrm{EN}}(s)\) computed from sealed validation values. | Correct and preserves P4’s primary outcome boundary. |
| Panel reading | Count eligible seeds classified as both / only-R / only-A / neither, plus ineligible parents; no mean, CI, p-value, seed drop, replacement, or early stop. | Excellent safeguard against outcome-driven aggregation. |

## Linkage to P1.1, P2, P3, and P4

P5 should not be described as a detached “P4 sequel.” It occupies a precise position in a cumulative protocol stack. Each earlier study supplies a different type of inherited constraint; none grants permission to alter its own preregistration retrospectively.

| Earlier study | What P5 inherits | What P5 must not inherit or claim | Correct linkage sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1.1** | The frozen WikiText-TL-39 canonical text and reconstructed 70/15/15 split, protected Tagalog validation/test boundaries, equal-budget discipline, full-split BPB method, final-checkpoint rule, and provenance-gate logic. | P1.1 weights, the P1.1 selected-depth conclusion as a P5 result, or P1.1 test BPB as a P5 observation. | “P5 reuses the P1.1-qualified Tagalog corpus and protected evaluation apparatus, not P1.1 model weights or outcomes.” |
| **P2** | The named WikiText-103-raw source, official English split identity, English test boundary, equal phase-two budget lineage, and active-control logic. | P2’s English BPE, English parent weights, A-arm results, or P2’s forward-direction conclusion as a P5 result. | “P5 inherits the canonical English stream provenance through the P2/P3/P4 apparatus, but it is not a P2 replication and does not load P2 weights.” |
| **P3** | The fresh-Tagalog-parent direction, P0-T before any English child token, C0 common-parent branching, the P3/P4 carry-forward Tagalog tokenizer, fresh-optimizer policy, validation-before-test sequencing, and C3-is-not-B3 warning. | P3 B3, P3 weights, P3 Gate V scores, or the claim that P5 is a direct replication of P3’s pure-English B2 contrast. | “P5 carries forward the reverse-direction parent and protected-evaluation architecture from P3, but replaces P3’s document-balanced B3 coordinate with the distinct P4 token-share treatment.” |
| **P4** | The direct treatment being replicated: fresh Tagalog parent, C1 extra Tagalog, C2 pure English, C3 locked \(q_{\mathrm{TL}}=0.50\) token-share mix, common budget, P4 tokenizer, P4 C3 manifest, P4 primary contrasts, C3-only secondary test policy, and C3-as-trade-off boundary. | P4 weights, P4 seed-0 result as a P5 panel cell, P4 numerical magnitudes as tuning targets, or any claim of an optimal/general mixture. | “P5 is an outcome-informed, multi-seed replication panel of P4’s token-share-locked treatment; P4 seed 0 is historical context, not a P5 confirmatory observation.” |

The causal and evidentiary progression is therefore:

> **P1.1** supplies a protected Tagalog measurement instrument; **P2** establishes the opposite-direction English-stream apparatus and a forward-direction dissociation; **P3** supplies a qualified fresh Tagalog parent and shows that a document-balanced mixture does not resolve exposure-definition ambiguity; **P4** converts that ambiguity into a frozen token-share treatment and observes its two branch contrasts once; **P5** asks whether the P4 pattern recurs across a predeclared set of new model-initialization trajectories.

This is a clean progression from measurement validity to parent provenance, directional branch contrasts, a defined mixture treatment, and then **seed-stability evidence**. P5 still does not establish a population parameter, a mechanism, mitigation efficacy, an optimal ratio, or external generalization.

## Major strengths

The proposed form already contains many of the safeguards a rigorous panel needs. These should remain exactly in the final PDF or in an addendum whose SHA-256 is named in the PDF.

| Strength | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| It says “It’s complicated” in the prior-data question and explains that P4 outcomes are known but no P5 outcome exists. | This is the right disclosure for an outcome-informed prospective replication. |
| Seeds 1, 2, and 3 are named before filing. | Prevents trying seeds until a desired result appears. |
| P4 seed 0 is explicitly historical and not a P5 confirmatory cell. | Prevents retrospective inflation of the P5 sample size from a differently timed study. |
| C3 reuses P4’s frozen stream by hash and disallows reshuffling. | Keeps the treatment fixed so the panel isolates parent initialization rather than data-order changes. |
| P0-T is required before any child token, and ineligible parents are retained in the panel record rather than replaced. | Prevents conditioning the panel on only favorable parents. |
| All seed-level validation and test scalars remain lockboxed until one panel Gate X. | Prevents seed-by-seed adaptation, informal stopping, and outcome-driven operations. |
| Every eligible seed receives C1/C2/C3, exactly 294 child steps, and only C3 is tested after its own validation seal. | Retains P4’s common-parent, equal-budget, and protected-secondary-test logic. |
| The panel reports a count table rather than a post-hoc mean or inferential model. | Matches the small, fixed panel and avoids overclaiming from three trajectories. |
| No early stop, no replacement seed, no new ratio, no post-outcome retuning of \(q_{\mathrm{TL}}\), \(D\), or \(\delta\). | Precommits against the most important outcome-dependent paths. |

## Required pre-filing corrections

These edits are recommended **before approval**, not after. They harden the operational interpretation of the already-proposed design; they do not add a treatment, change the hypotheses, or amend P1.1–P4.

### 1. Define the initialization-seed mechanism, not just the seed labels

The form currently names “parent-init seed \(s\)” and says the same \(s\) is used at d8 and d20, but does not identify the exact implementation pathway that makes `s=1`, `s=2`, and `s=3` generate different parent initial weights. This is the most important technical ambiguity.

Add a concise sentence such as:

> “For each filed parent seed \(s\in\{1,2,3\}\), the P5-owned launch wrapper sets and records the trainer’s model-initialization RNG state for Python, NumPy, PyTorch CPU, and PyTorch CUDA before model construction; the wrapper SHA and resulting initial-state/checkpoint identity are recorded. No validation, test, C3-order, or branch-selection decision uses \(s\).”

If the pinned nanochat trainer exposes a specific seed flag, name that flag and the P5 wrapper rather than relying on an implied seed convention. If it does not, the P5-owned wrapper must be the filed mechanism. The C3 interleave remains separately fixed at 42; it is **not** a parent seed.

### 2. Make the P0-T untrained-floor policy seed-matched or explicitly fixed

The form currently says “Untrained P0-T floor seed 0.” That is deterministic, but it is less natural for a fresh-parent seed panel because a parent initialized at seed 1, 2, or 3 is compared with an untrained model initialized at a different seed.

The preferred policy is:

> “For each \(s\), P0-T compares the d8 and d20 P5 parent against untrained same-depth models initialized with the same filed seed \(s\), plus the shared Tagalog train-fitted add-one byte-unigram floor. All floor evaluations are full validation, CUDA-only, and are lockboxed to the status `PASS` or `BLOCKED` before panel unblinding.”

This does not change the P0-T construct; it simply makes the same-depth untrained comparator coherent with the one factor intentionally varied across P5. If the authors deliberately prefer a single fixed seed-0 untrained reference, retain it only with an explicit justification and make that a frozen policy in the addendum. Do not leave the point implicit.

### 3. State that scalar values from P0-T, Gate U, and Gate V are inaccessible until panel Gate X

“No seed-level unblinding” is excellent, but it should also specify where logs, evaluator JSON, stdout, and test reports go. Otherwise a user could avoid formally opening a lockbox while still seeing a scalar in a terminal log.

Add:

> “Before panel Gate X, permitted P0-T output is only `PASS`, `BLOCKED`, or `TECHNICAL_STOP`; Gate U/V scalar files, evaluator stdout/stderr, and test reports are written directly to the P5 lockbox and are not displayed, copied into public run cards, or inspected. `test_access[s]` is a safe count, not a score disclosure.”

### 4. Replace “exclude a run” with a status taxonomy

The exclusions paragraph correctly lists integrity failures, but “exclude” can sound as if completed unfavorable results might later be discarded. Use a clearer distinction:

| Event | Correct P5 disposition |
|---|---|
| Hash mismatch, forbidden weight lineage, test/validation contamination, altered tokenizer, altered C3 quota/order, `ratio=-1`, non-CUDA official gate, or a child used as a parent | **Protocol stop for the affected seed or study**; preserve receipts and do not treat an output as a P5 result. |
| Pre-outcome technical failure with no official terminal checkpoint or no accessed P5 scalar | **Quarantine the failed attempt** and restart the official branch from the same immutable \(C0_s\), fresh optimizer, and new empty output directory; log the incident. |
| Finite terminal official checkpoint and sealed scalar | **Never exclude for outcome.** Retain it in the filed panel classification even if it fails both criteria. |
| P0-T blocked | **Ineligible seed**; no child training, no replacement seed, but remaining predeclared seeds continue. |

### 5. Specify the exact panel execution and release order

The form nearly does this, but the protocol should make the no-adaptation sequence mechanically unambiguous:

> “Run the predeclared seed order 1, then 2, then 3. For an eligible seed, perform R→S→T, Gate U, then one C3-only Gate V. All score-bearing outputs remain lockboxed. Continue through every filed seed despite any safe operational status from preceding seeds. Execute exactly one panel Gate X only after each seed has either completed V or reached the filed P0-T ineligible stop.”

This makes the term “one panel unblinding” auditable and makes clear that P5 has up to three secondary C3 test events—**one per eligible seed**, predeclared—not one total test call for the entire panel.

### 6. Use P5-owned scripts and an exact host/software identity

The form should state `scripts/p5/env.sh`, `scripts/p5/env.cuda.sh`, P5-owned frozen-parent continuation wrapper, and `scripts/p5/evaluate_bpb.py`; it should not use P4 environment files. P4 code may be copied as an auditable starting point, but P5 must own its run configuration and record the P5 script hashes.

For the most faithful P4 replication, replace “A40 class” with the exact intended hardware/software contract if feasible: NVIDIA A40 48 GB, CUDA 12.8, Torch 2.9.1, and the stated container image. If a hardware substitution is permitted, define it prospectively together with the receipt and technical-stop rules. A vague “A40 class” label allows unnecessary numerical and operational variance.

### 7. Tighten the study-type and independence language

“Observational / archival data study” is understandable because the text corpora are frozen public datasets, but the actual operation is **prospectively controlled computational training and evaluation**. If the form offers a more accurate non-human computational/other category, choose it. If it does not, retain observational/archival and state in Q8 that no human participants are assigned and P5’s trained weights and evaluation values are newly generated.

The form’s “independent” designation for the unrelated Myers–Perry study is not a concern. For P1.1–P4, however, P5 is intentionally linked through shared frozen corpora, tokenizer artifacts, and treatment specifications. The correct language is:

> “P5 is computationally independent of P4 in its parent weights, children, ledgers, and P5 outcomes; it is not outcome-independent of P4, because P4’s released seed-0 results informed this pre-filed replication panel.”

### 8. Make the P1.1/P2/P3/P4 authority table explicit in the addendum

The form already points to a SHA-bound addendum. Add a one-table inheritance registry with three columns: `carried forward unchanged`, `fresh by P5 design`, and `prohibited`. This is the cleanest way to make the program linkage reviewable without increasing the form beyond two pages.

| Carried forward unchanged | Fresh by P5 design | Prohibited |
|---|---|---|
| Code pin, canonical data manifests, P3/P4 tokenizer pair, C3 packed stream/origin mask, budget, T, evaluator, thresholds, C3-only test policy | P5 seed panel, P5 parents/children, optimizer states, output paths, lockbox, gate cards, terminal checkpoints, test-access ledger | P1.1/P2/P3/P4 weights as P5 parents, P4 seed 0 as a P5 cell, new tokenizer, new mixture ratio, byte balancing, altered stream order, re-cleaned corpus, SFT, replay, EWC, post-outcome extra seed, P1–P4 environment files |

## Optional presentation edits

The draft is concise for the information it must lock. A two-page AsPredicted PDF is appropriate here; shrinking it to one page by removing seed identities, hash-bound treatment definition, test-access controls, or no-replacement rules would make the registration weaker.

Small wording improvements are nevertheless available. In Q2, use “two-component trade-off pattern” instead of “joint trade-off” when defining the both-criteria event; this avoids implying a composite statistic. In Q5, replace “not a population effect” with “not a population estimate, confidence interval, or inferential test,” which is more precise. In Q6, change “exclude a run” to the status taxonomy above. In Q8, add the direct statement that P5 is the **separate multi-seed filing anticipated by P4’s limitation statement**.

## Suggested concise linkage paragraph for Question 8

> “P5 is the separately preregistered multi-seed follow-up identified by P4’s one-seed limitation. P1.1 supplies the protected Tagalog corpus/split and BPB measurement apparatus; P2 supplies the canonical WikiText-103-raw stream provenance and matched-branch lineage; P3 supplies the fresh-Tagalog-parent, P0-T, common-parent, and protected-evaluation architecture; P4 supplies the distinct token-share-locked C3 treatment, contrasts, and C3-only test policy. P5 changes only the predeclared parent-initialization seed and newly trained descendants. It does not load earlier weights, count P4 seed 0 as a P5 cell, or treat P4’s released magnitudes as calibration targets.”

## Final decision

The proposed P5 is a good registration and a logical successor to P1.1–P4. **Its strongest scientific feature is that it does not let P4’s observed seed-0 success decide whether to stop, replace, discard, or reconfigure the new seeds.** File the panel only after the seed-RNG mechanism, per-seed P0-T floor policy, panel-wide scalar quarantine, technical-failure taxonomy, P5-owned script identity, and exact host policy are made explicit in the form or SHA-bound addendum.

Once those items are frozen, the study cleanly asks: not whether P4 “worked” once, but whether its two predeclared token-share trade-off patterns recur across three new, fully specified model trajectories.
