2) Hypothesis
================================================================
This study was designed after P2 Gate U/V unblinding on 19 August 2026 (AsPredicted #306935). It is a post-P2, prospectively preregistered reverse-direction study. It is not an outcome-independent mirror of P2, not confirmation of P2, and does not amend #306780 or #306935.

After a newly trained P3-specific Tagalog nanochat parent (TL0), initialized from fresh weights and trained only on the frozen P1.1 WikiText-TL-39 train documents under a new P3 Tagalog tokenizer, passes P0-T, does English continuation on WikiText-103 raise Tagalog held-out BPB more than matched extra Tagalog?

C_tl = Tagalog val_bpb_full(B2) - Tagalog val_bpb_full(B1). Predict C_tl >= 0.01 at d20. C_tl is the declared English stream vs the declared extra-Tagalog stream (equal model-visible tokens), not pure language identity.

G_en = English val_bpb_full(B2) - English val_bpb_full(B1). Predict G_en <= -0.01. B0 to B2 English change is descriptive only.

B3 is a 50/50 document mix trade-off arm, not token-equated mitigation. Report C_tl(B3)=TL(B3)-TL(B1) and G_en(B3)=EN(B3)-EN(B1), with realized document, byte, and P3-Tagalog-BPE token shares. Smaller C_tl(B3) alone is not mitigation.

P0-T, before any English train token: TL0 Tagalog val_bpb_full is >= 0.01 below both an untrained same-depth model and a train-fitted add-one UTF-8 byte unigram on the same Tagalog val bytes, at BOTH depth 8 and depth 20. If either depth fails either floor, do not train B1/B2/B3; report a blocked parent-eligibility result; do not call the parent Tagalog-pretrained and do not interpret B2 as reverse CF.

Do not load P1.1 or P2 weights as parent. Same P3 Tagalog 32768 BPE for every P3 arm. Not CORE/chat/SFT.
