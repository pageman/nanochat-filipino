\begin{abstract}

mechanism'' suppressing Weyl Type~D deviations by \(10^6\) in equal-spin
Myers--Perry (MP) black holes, an ``accelerated fragility'' coefficient

This revision enumerates six layers of weakness of that draft; writes

Proposition--Lemma form with Lamport-style hierarchical proofs, the
statements that survive; stress-tests the repaired claims in fifty
de-duplicated ways; and records a before/after ledger. No quarantined
number is replaced by a new fiction. This manuscript does not amend
nanochat-filipino studies AsPredicted \#306780 or \#306935.

\section{Standing definitions}

\subsection{Myers--Perry parameters and cohomogeneity}

Let \(D=2n+\varepsilon\) with \(\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}\) and
\(n=\lfloor(D-1)/2\rfloor\). The vacuum Myers--Perry metric is
parameterized by a mass \(M>0\) and \(n\) spin parameters
\(a_i\in\mathbb{R}\)~\cite{MyersPerry1986}. Its generic isometry algebra
is \(\mathbb{R}_t\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)^{\oplus n}\).

\begin{definition}[Generic cohomogeneity]
The generic cohomogeneity of MP is
\(\operatorname{cohom}_{\mathrm{gen}}(D):=D-n-1=n+\varepsilon-1\).
Thus \(\operatorname{cohom}_{\mathrm{gen}}(5)=2\) and
\(\operatorname{cohom}_{\mathrm{gen}}(6)=3\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Equal-spin locus]
The equal-spin locus is \(\{a_1=\cdots=a_n\}\). For odd \(D=2n+1\) this
enhances the isometry to \(\mathbb{R}_t\times U(n)\) and drops the
cohomogeneity to \(1\)~\cite{GibbonsLuPagePope2004}. For even \(D\) the
equal-spin enhancement does \emph{not} reproduce that cohomogeneity-1
story; v1's uniform ``\(D=5\)--\(9\) cohomogeneity-1'' clause is false.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Subextremal / ultraspinning]
In \(D=5\) there is a Kerr-like bound on \((a_1,a_2)\) at fixed \(M\);
there is no ultraspinning regime. In \(D\ge6\) some spin directions are
unbounded and the Emparan--Myers ultraspinning instability
exists~\cite{EmparanMyers2003,DiasFiguerasMonteiroSantos2010}.
v1's \(D=5\) caption ``as \(a\to1\)'' is therefore either a silent
normalization \(a/a_{\mathrm{ext}}\) or an extrapolation off the
black-hole domain.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Principal tensor}

\begin{definition}[Closed conformal Killing--Yano 2-form]
A 2-form \(h\) is a \emph{closed conformal Killing--Yano} (CKY) tensor
if \(\mathrm{d}h=0\) and \(\nabla_X h=X^\flat\wedge\xi\) for a 1-form
\(\xi\) (necessarily \(\xi=\frac{1}{D-1}\delta h\)). It is
\emph{principal} if it is nondegenerate as a 2-form (maximal matrix
rank \(2n\)).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Killing--Yano residual]
For a trial 2-form \(h\) on a metric \(g\),
\[
\mathcal{R}(h;g):=\bigl\|\nabla_X h-X^\flat\wedge\xi\bigr\|_{L^2(K)}
\]
on a specified compact region \(K\) outside the horizon, in a specified
frame. v1 never wrote this residual. No v1 number called a ``KY
residual'' is used here.
\end{definition}

The existence of a principal tensor on MP, and the generation of a
Killing tower of \(n\) independent Killing tensors together with
\(n+\varepsilon\) Killing vectors, is the theorem of
Frolov--Kubiz\v{n}\'ak--Krtou\v{s}--Page~\cite{FrolovKubiznak2007,Krtousetal2007,FrolovKrtousKubiznak2017}.

\subsection{CMPP type}

\begin{definition}
The Coley--Milson--Pravda--Pravdov\'a (CMPP) classification assigns an
algebraic type to the Weyl tensor via boost-weight
alignment~\cite{Coleyetal2004}. MP is CMPP Type~D. A principal CKY
2-form implies Type~D. The converse in \(D\ge5\) is not a
theorem~\cite{FrolovKrtousKubiznak2017}.
\end{definition}

\subsection{EFT truncation (classical, not semiclassical)}

\begin{definition}[Quadratic vacuum EFT in \(D\ge5\)]
Up to total derivatives and field redefinitions of the metric that
preserve the Einstein frame at leading order,
\[
S[g]=\frac{1}{16\pi G_D}\int\mathrm{d}^D x\sqrt{-g}
\Bigl(R+\alpha_{\mathrm{GB}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GB}}
+\alpha_{\mathrm{W}}\,C_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}C^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}+\cdots\Bigr),
\]
where \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GB}}\) is Gauss--Bonnet. Ricci-squared
operators are redundant on Einstein backgrounds at this order.
Gauss--Bonnet is topological in \(D=4\) and dynamical in \(D\ge5\).
There is no \(\hbar\) in \(S[g]\).
\end{definition}

Generic quadratic curvature without a Lovelock or reduction-of-order
treatment introduces Ostrogradsky ghosts. Reall--Santos Euclidean
thermodynamics does not supply a corrected
metric~\cite{ReallSantos2019}. KY residuals require a metric.

\begin{definition}[EFT validity]
A first-order deformation \(g=g_{\mathrm{MP}}+\alpha\,\delta g+O(\alpha^2)\)
is inside the EFT if \(|\alpha\,\mathrm{Riem}[g_{\mathrm{MP}}]|\ll1\) on
and outside the outer horizon.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Love numbers and quarantine}

In \(D\ge5\), generic static scalar Love numbers of MP are

\section{Formal development}

Lamport-style proofs are indented hierarchies. A step \(\langle k\rangle m\)
is the \(m\)-th claim at level \(k\). Citations to the literature are

\subsection{Discrete identities}

\begin{lemma}[Killing-tower cardinality]
\label{lem:tower}
Let \(D=2n+\varepsilon\) with \(\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}\). If a principal
CKY 2-form exists, the Killing tower produces \(n\) Killing tensors and
\(n+\varepsilon\) Killing vectors, totaling \(D\) constants of geodesic
motion.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) \(n+(n+\varepsilon)=2n+\varepsilon\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle1.\ \) \(n+(n+\varepsilon)=(n+n)+\varepsilon\) by
associativity in \(\mathbb{N}\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle2.\ \) \(n+n=2n\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle3.\ \) \(2n+\varepsilon=D\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle4.\ \) QED.\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) Geometric generation of those tensors and
vectors is the Killing-tower construction, used as an external
lemma~\cite{Krtousetal2007}.\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

A Mathlib-free Lean encoding of \(\langle1\rangle1\) is
\texttt{killing\_tower\_count} in \texttt{lean/KillingTower.lean}.

\begin{lemma}[Spin count]
\label{lem:nspins}
The number of independent MP spins is \(n=\lfloor(D-1)/2\rfloor\).
In particular \(n(5)=2\), \(n(6)=2\), \(n(7)=3\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) If \(D=2k+1\) then \((D-1)/2=k\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) If \(D=2k\) with \(k\ge1\) then
\((D-1)//2=k-1\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) \(D=5=2\cdot2+1\) gives \(n=2\); \(D=6=2\cdot3\)
gives \(n=2\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Equal-spin measure]
\label{lem:measure}
For \(n\ge2\) the equal-spin locus has codimension \(n-1\ge1\) in spin
space, hence measure zero in any absolutely continuous sampling measure
(including Sobol on the unit cube).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) \(\{a_1=\cdots=a_n\}\subset\mathbb{R}^n\) is a

\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) Codimension \(n-1\ge1\) since \(n\ge2\) for all

\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) A Sobol sequence on the full cube almost surely

\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Logical independence on the MP family}

Let \(A\) be ``CMPP Type~D,'' \(B\) ``admits a principal CKY 2-form
(hence completely integrable geodesic flow),'' \(C\) ``linearly stable
to gravitational perturbations about the outer horizon,'' and
\(D_{\mathrm{cc}}\) ``the spacetime is a black hole obeying weak cosmic
censorship.''

\begin{proposition}[Pairwise independence]
\label{prop:indep}
The four properties \(A,B,C,D_{\mathrm{cc}}\) are not mutually
equivalent. In particular \(B\Rightarrow C\), \(B\Rightarrow D_{\mathrm{cc}}\),
\(A\Rightarrow D_{\mathrm{cc}}\), and \(A\Rightarrow B\) (in \(D\ge5\))
are not theorems.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) \(A\wedge B\) holds for every subextremal MP
metric.\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle1.\ \) MP admits a principal CKY
2-form~\cite{FrolovKubiznak2007}. That is \(B\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle2.\ \) A principal CKY 2-form implies CMPP
Type~D~\cite{FrolovKrtousKubiznak2017}. That is \(A\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle3.\ \) QED.\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) \(B\not\Rightarrow C\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle1.\ \) Geodesic integrability holds for all MP
parameters for which the metric is defined, including ultraspinning
\(D\ge6\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle2.\ \) Ultraspinning MP black holes are linearly
unstable~\cite{EmparanMyers2003,Diasetal2010,DiasFiguerasMonteiroSantos2010}.
Equal-spin \(D=9\) in particular has exponentially growing modes.\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle3.\ \) Therefore there exist MP spacetimes with
\(B\wedge\neg C\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle4.\ \) QED.\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) \(B\not\Rightarrow D_{\mathrm{cc}}\) and
\(A\not\Rightarrow D_{\mathrm{cc}}\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle1.\ \) Super-extremal Kerr (\(D=4\)) and
super-extremal MP (\(D=5\), beyond the Kerr-like bound) remain Type~D
and admit the same principal tensor. That is \(A\wedge B\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle2.\ \) Those geometries have naked singularities.
That is \(\neg D_{\mathrm{cc}}\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle3.\ \) QED.\\
\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) \(A\Rightarrow B\) is not a theorem in
\(D\ge5\).\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle1.\ \) In \(D=4\), vacuum Type~D is Kerr--NUT,
which does admit a principal CKY.\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle2.\ \) In \(D\ge5\) the converse is explicitly
open~\cite{FrolovKrtousKubiznak2017}. v1 used the converse silently
whenever it treated ``Weyl Type~D deviation'' as a proxy for KY
breakdown.\\
\quad\(\langle2\rangle3.\ \) QED.\\
\(\langle1\rangle5.\ \) Therefore v1's chain ``EFT lifts Type~D
\(\Rightarrow\) KY dies \(\Rightarrow\) integrability dies
\(\Rightarrow\) naked singularities, faster than GR'' contains three
non-implications.\\
\(\langle1\rangle6.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

Algebraic ``fragility'' is not a censorship theorem and is not a

\subsection{Equal-spin protection is classical isometry}

\begin{lemma}[Quadrature from cohomogeneity one]
\label{lem:quad}
Let \((M,g)\) be Lorentzian of cohomogeneity one, with compact spacelike
orbits of a group \(G\) acting by isometries, and with orbit space an
interval in \(r\). Then geodesic motion reduces to quadrature in \(r\)
after using the conserved charges of \(G\), without any principal CKY
tensor.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) Isometries yield \(\dim G\) linear conserved
momenta \(p_G\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) The metric depends on a single coordinate \(r\).
The Hamiltonian constraint becomes a first-order ODE
\(\dot r^2=V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r;p_G,m)\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) A first-order autonomous ODE in one variable is
solvable by quadrature.\\
\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) No CKY equation entered
\(\langle1\rangle1\)--\(\langle1\rangle3\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle5.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Odd-\(D\) equal-spin MP is cohomogeneity one]
\label{lem:odd}
For \(D=2n+1\) with \(a_1=\cdots=a_n\), MP has cohomogeneity one.
Hence Lemma~\ref{lem:quad} applies already at EFT coupling \(\alpha=0\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) The isometry is enhanced to
\(\mathbb{R}_t\times U(n)\)~\cite{GibbonsLuPagePope2004}.\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) The metric functions depend only on \(r\).\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[No \(\hbar\) in the quadratic action]
\label{lem:hbar}
Definition~2.7's action does not contain \(\hbar\). Any protection that
follows from \(G\)-invariance of a deformation of that action is
classical.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) Inspect \(S[g]\): the symbols are \(g_{\mu\nu}\),

\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) \(\hbar\) is not among them.\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[What equal-spin protection actually is]
\label{prop:protect}
Let \(g(\alpha)=g_{\mathrm{MP}}+\alpha\,\delta g+O(\alpha^2)\) preserve
the enhanced equal-spin isometry \(G=\mathbb{R}_t\times U(n)\) in odd
\(D\). Then: (1)~ \(g(\alpha)\) remains cohomogeneity one, so geodesic
quadrature persists whether or not a principal CKY tensor persists;
(2)~Weyl components in nontrivial \(G\)-representations are forbidden
exactly, not up to \(10^{-6}\); (3)~the CKY equation is a separate PDE;
(4)~none of this is semiclassical.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) If \(\mathcal{L}_K g(\alpha)=0\) for all Killing
fields of \(G\), Lemma~\ref{lem:quad} applies to \(g(\alpha)\).
Persistence of quadrature is cheaper than persistence of \(h\). v1's
``deformed Killing--Yano tensor remains nearly invariant'' is a
stronger unproved PDE claim.\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) Decompose Weyl into \(G\)-irreps. A \(G\)-invariant
metric has Weyl tensor in the singlet. Non-singlet boost-weight
components vanish identically. A reported \(10^6\) suppression is
therefore a numerical floor or evidence the configuration was not
exactly equal-spin.\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) The CKY equation is overdetermined. Isometry
reduces the unknown functions; it does not satisfy the remaining radial
ODE automatically.\\
\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) Statement~(4) is Lemma~\ref{lem:hbar}.\\
\(\langle1\rangle5.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}

\subsection{EFT, ghosts, thermodynamics, and singularities}

\begin{lemma}[Field-redefinition redundancy]
On an Einstein background, Ricci-squared operators can be removed by a
metric field redefinition at leading order in \(\alpha\). A pipeline
that ``turns on \(R^2\)'' without specifying Gauss--Bonnet versus
Weyl-squared versus redundant Ricci-squared has not specified an
EFT~\cite{ReallSantos2019,CanoRuiperez2019}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Ostrogradsky]
A generic \(R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\) term without
Lovelock structure or reduction of order yields extra ghost modes. An
observed ``fragility'' of such a system may be an artifact of those
modes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Reall--Santos does not give KY residuals]
The Reall--Santos method computes first-order thermodynamic corrections
without constructing \(\delta g_{\mu\nu}\)~\cite{ReallSantos2019}. The
CKY residual is a tensor constructed from \(g\). Therefore RS output
cannot be the Label Engine of v1.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Truncated EFT does not resolve \(r=0\)]
\label{lem:sing}
A finite-order curvature expansion is an infrared expansion about a GR
solution. It is not a UV completion and does not replace the curvature
singularity of MP by a regular core.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Lamport-style]
\(\langle1\rangle1.\ \) By definition of a truncated EFT, operators of
dimension \(>N\) are omitted.\\
\(\langle1\rangle2.\ \) Near a curvature singularity, omitted operators
are not small.\\
\(\langle1\rangle3.\ \) Therefore the truncation cannot be used
\emph{at} the singularity.\\
\(\langle1\rangle4.\ \) Nonperturbative RG improvement is a different
theory, not a theorem of the \(R^2\) truncation. v1's conclusion that
``perturbative truncations are insufficient for singularity resolution''
is a tautology of the action, not a numerical discovery.\\
\(\langle1\rangle5.\ \) QED.
\end{proof}
