2) Hypothesis
================================================================
This study was designed after released P3 findings and after the P3 B3 document/byte-share ambiguity were known (P3 Gate X 2026-08-20; #307342). It is a post-P3 token-share mixture trade-off preregistration. It does not amend #306780, #306935, or #307342. It is not "P3 B3 fixed."

After a newly trained P4 Tagalog nanochat parent (fresh weights; frozen P1.1 WikiText-TL-39 train; carry-forward P3 Tagalog 32768 BPE) passes P0-T, freeze d20 as C0. Continue C0 for one shared token budget on C1 extra Tagalog, C2 pure English, and C3 a pre-frozen EN/TL mix with q_TL=0.50 Tagalog source-content tokens under the carry-forward P3 tokenizer (no BOS/pad/pack/crop). Does C3 retain more Tagalog than C2 while still acquiring more English than C1?

R_TL = TL val_bpb_full(C3) - TL val_bpb_full(C2). Success: R_TL <= -0.01.
A_EN = EN val_bpb_full(C3) - EN val_bpb_full(C1). Success: A_EN <= -0.01.
Equality at -0.01 counts. Both required for the joint trade-off. No composite. P4 does not claim byte balancing.

P0-T, before any child token, at BOTH d8 and d20: Tagalog val_bpb_full is >= 0.01 below untrained same-depth and below a train-fitted add-1 UTF-8 byte unigram. If either depth fails either floor, do not train C1/C2/C3. d8 is eligibility only; d20 is the only child parent.

Do not load P1.1, P2, or P3 weights. Not CORE/chat/SFT. One seed. Mitigation is not claimed here.
